VOLANT GLOBAL CARGO

Cargo Services

Fast, secure, and temperature-controlled air freight solutions connecting major international industries.

Global Logistics Infrastructure

Volant Cargo operates a fleet of dedicated Boeing 777 Freighters and utilizes the cargo belly capacity of all commercial passenger flights. This allows us to offer express shipping, secure transit, and bulk cargo solutions to over 200 airports worldwide.

Our primary cargo hubs are located at Tokyo Narita (NRT) and Hazrat Shahjalal (DAC), handling regular cargo flights for global supply chains.

Specialized Air Cargo Solutions

  • PharmaTemp: Active temperature-controlled containers for vaccines and life sciences.
  • Volant SafePet: Climate-controlled, ventilated shipping areas for family pets and livestock.
  • Secured Vault: Reinforced container transit for high-value cargo, artwork, and precious metals.

ACCESSIBLE TRAVEL FOR ALL

Special Assistance

Request wheelchair services, medical clearance guidelines, and register unaccompanied minor travels.

Mobility & Wheelchair Requests

Volant Airlines provides comprehensive terminal assistance for passengers with reduced mobility. We offer ramp wheelchairs, cabin wheelchairs, and help with boarding/deplaning at Haneda, Narita, Dhaka, and all other destination terminals.

You can request wheelchair assistance during your booking process, or by contacting our hotline at least 48 hours prior to your flight departure.

Unaccompanied Minors (UM)

Children aged 5 to 11 traveling alone are eligible for our dedicated Unaccompanied Minor service. Our trained staff will accompany the child from check-in, guide them through security and passport control, look after them during the flight, and safely hand them over to the designated guardian at the destination airport.

Medical Clearances

Passengers traveling with complex medical conditions, requiring oxygen concentrators, or in late stages of pregnancy may require a Medical Information Form (MEDIF) completed by their doctor. Please download the MEDIF form or contact our customer support at {{ contactPhone }} for more assistance.

General Baggage Rules

  • Cabin Baggage: 1 personal item (fits under the seat) + 1 overhead cabin bag (max 56 x 36 x 23 cm, max 10kg total).
  • Restricted Items: Lithium batteries, aerosols, matches, and flammable liquids are strictly prohibited in checked baggage.
  • Sporting Equipment: Bicycles, golf sets, and ski equipment are counted as standard check-in bags if they are under 23kg and 158cm total dimensions.

Lost or Delayed Baggage

If your checked baggage did not arrive on your flight, please file a report at the airport Baggage Service Desk immediately. You will receive a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) and code. For online updates, you can email our baggage desk at baggage@volantairlines.com.
